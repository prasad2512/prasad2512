### 1. 🛕 Kumbh Connect — Real-Time Pilgrimage & Crowd Management Platform
* **Platform:** Mobile Application (Flutter, Dart) & Scalable Cloud Backend (Node.js, Express, MongoDB)
* **Domain:** Mass Crowd Navigation, Emergency Response, Smart Tourism & Public Safety
* **Key Architecture & Capabilities:**
  * **Interactive GIS Mapping:** Real-time location tracking of key ghats, amenities, medical camps, and transit hubs.
  * **Crowd Density & Route Optimization:** Live telemetry and route diversion algorithms to mitigate stampede risks and congestion.
  * **Emergency SOS & Broadcast Engine:** Low-latency push notification pipeline delivering high-priority safety advisories and missing persons alerts.
  * **Offline-First Resilience:** Local caching ensures uninterrupted access to maps and emergency helplines in low-connectivity zones.

### 2. 🤖 Astra — Enterprise Self-Hosted AI & RAG Engine
* **Platform:** Full-Stack AI Platform (Python, Node.js, React, Docker)
* **Domain:** Offline Generative AI, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) & Local LLMs
* **Key Architecture & Capabilities:**
  * **Air-Gapped & Private:** Fully self-hosted inference engine supporting **Ollama, llama.cpp**, and OpenAI-compatible endpoints with zero external data leakage.
  * **Hybrid Document Ingestion:** Intelligent chunking and embedding pipelines for enterprise PDFs, documentation, and source code.
  * **Vector Database Integration:** High-speed semantic similarity retrieval providing verified context directly into prompt windows.
